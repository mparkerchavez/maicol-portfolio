01 /// The Challenge

Separating Signal from Noise.

In the rapidly evolving landscape of Generative AI, enterprise leadership is often bombarded with hype. The challenge wasn't a lack of information, but a lack of context.

How do we distinguish between a flashy demo and a technology that will fundamentally reshape our business model in 3 years? We needed a systematic way to evaluate emerging tech against our specific enterprise constraints.

02 /// The Strategy

A Repeatable Research System.

I developed a repeatable research system to accelerate synthesis. This wasn't just about reading news; it was about translating technical capabilities into business implications.

  • Horizon 1 (Now): What can we deploy today to solve immediate friction?
  • Horizon 2 (Next): What pilots need to start now to scale in 18 months?
  • Horizon 3 (Future): What disruptive shifts must we prepare our infrastructure for?

This framework allowed us to move from reactive panic to proactive planning.
