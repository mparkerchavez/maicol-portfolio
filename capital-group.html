01 /// The Challenge

The "Blank Page" Problem.

Capital Group's sales teams spend hours prepping for client meetings. They needed to digest complex portfolio data and client history in minutes.

Data Science had built a powerful backend, but the frontend was a generic "Chat with your Data" interface. Adoption was stalled because sales associates didn't know what to ask. They were staring at a blank cursor.

02 /// The Strategy

Validating the Workflow, Not Just the Tech.

Instead of guessing features, I led a rapid validation sprint. We didn't write code initially. I sat with sales associates and manually mapped their "meeting prep" mental model.

  • Insight 1: Sales reps don't want to "chat." They want a dossier.
  • Insight 2: Trust is fragile. One hallucination kills adoption.

I pivoted the team from a "Chatbot" to a "Context-Aware Agent" that pre-emptively surfaces the 3 most likely questions a client will ask, sourced from valid RAG data.
