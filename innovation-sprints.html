01 /// The Challenge

Where Should We Apply AI?

As Generative AI emerged, teams across the enterprise had the same question: "How do we identify where AI can actually add value to our workflows?"

The challenge wasn't technical capability—it was decision clarity. Teams needed a structured way to move from broad AI enthusiasm to specific, validated use cases aligned with real business problems.

02 /// The Approach

Structured Discovery Through Innovation Sprints.

I adapted the Design Sprint methodology into a repeatable 2-week Innovation Sprint process optimized for enterprise scale and AI discovery.

  • Week 1 - Problem Framing: Facilitated cross-functional workshops to identify friction points, map user workflows, and define success criteria.
  • Week 2 - Validation: Rapid prototyping and testing with real users to validate assumptions before committing resources.
  • Output: Executive-ready decision summaries making assumptions, tradeoffs, and next actions explicit.

This wasn't just facilitation—it was decision acceleration. I enabled diverse stakeholders to align on problem statements, validate or invalidate concepts, and commit to clear next steps.
