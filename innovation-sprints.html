01 /// The Challenge

From AI Curiosity to Business-Ready Use Cases

As interest in AI accelerated, teams across the client experience space surfaced many ideas, but the hardest part was turning that enthusiasm into business-ready use cases. Opportunities were often framed as "can we use AI here?" rather than grounded in user needs, measurable outcomes, and clear constraints. Without a shared structure for prioritization and alignment, it was difficult for leaders and teams to decide what to pursue, what to defer, and how to connect initiatives to business outcomes.

02 /// The Strategy

A Repeatable Sprint System for AI Discovery and Alignment

I used innovation sprints as a repeatable way to move teams from AI ideas to decisions. Across 20+ sprints, my approach paired human-centered problem framing with an enterprise lens, helping teams define where AI could add value and what would matter for adoption.

I designed two AI-focused workshops to scale that approach:

  • Cross-functional AI discovery workshop (Sales, Marketing, Client Experience): brought together 30+ participants to empathize with users, identify high-priority opportunities, and produce a prioritized list of AI opportunities grounded in real customer and associate needs.
  • Senior leader alignment workshop: helped leaders build a cohesive AI strategy by mapping a lineage from AI initiatives to prioritized business outcomes, clarifying how use cases supported shared goals and where to focus investment.

These workshops created a shared language for AI use case framing and helped teams move forward with clearer priorities, alignment, and next steps.

03 /// The Outcome

Faster Alignment, Faster Decisions

The sprint approach helped teams and leaders move faster from open questions to clear decisions. Instead of debating AI in the abstract, the workshops created shared context around user needs, priority opportunities, and what success should look like, which reduced stalled conversations and accelerated next-step planning.

By combining cross-functional AI discovery with senior leader alignment to business outcomes, teams were able to converge on what to pursue, what to sequence later, and how initiatives connected to prioritized outcomes. This improved momentum and decision quality while maintaining an enterprise lens on responsible, adoptable AI.
